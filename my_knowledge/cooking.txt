# THE LOGISTICS OF FLAVOR: Kitchen Notes

## Core Recipes
* Quick Pasta: Use plenty of garlic, good olive oil, and the pasta water is the "liquid gold" that binds the sauce. Never rinse the pasta.
* Traditional Zeamă: The key is the sourness. Use homemade "borș" if possible. It’s the ultimate comfort food for a rainy day in Moldova.

## Flavor Science
* Maillard Reaction: The chemical reaction between amino acids and reducing sugars that gives browned food its distinctive flavor. This is why searing meat is non-negotiable.
* Salt timing: Salting steak 45 minutes before cooking allows the salt to draw out moisture, dissolve, and then be reabsorbed into the meat.

## Kitchen Gear
* I prefer my cast iron skillet for almost everything. It takes time to "season," but the heat retention is unmatched. 
* My dream kitchen would have a dedicated station for making handmade pasta.
