# TRAVEL NOTES: Denmark & Beyond (2025-2026)

## Move Preparation: Denmark
* Geography: Denmark is the southernmost of the Scandinavian countries. It consists of the Jutland peninsula and over 400 named islands.
* Transport: Get the "Rejsekort" (travel card) immediately upon arrival in Copenhagen. It works for buses, trains, and the metro.
* Housing Tip: Many Danish apartments are rented without light fixtures in the ceiling. I need to remember to buy lamps or "pendel" lights.
* Culture: "Hygge" isn't just a word; it's a feeling of coziness and soul-comfort. It’s why lighting and candles are so important in Danish homes.

## The History Bucket List
* Mycenae (Greece): I am fascinated by the Lion Gate and the "Cyclopean" walls—legend says only giants could move stones that big. 
* Knossos (Crete): The Minoan civilization's architecture feels so much more "modern" and airy compared to the heavy Mycenaean style.

## Travel Preferences
* I avoid tourist traps. I’d rather find a small "cafenea" in Chișinău or a local bakery in Aarhus.
* Always pack: My ROG Ally (for the long flights) and noise-canceling headphones.
